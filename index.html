Important limitation

PDCAAS is ingredient- and processing-specific. A branded food cannot be assigned an exact score from its nutrition label alone. Mixed foods require the amino-acid profile and digestibility of the full protein blend.

The FAO has recommended DIAAS as a more informative successor because it evaluates digestible essential amino acids individually at the end of the small intestine.

Starter database based on cited studies and official FAO methodology. Values are representative, not regulatory calculations for a specific product.
